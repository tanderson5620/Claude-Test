Donut Town VR

Catch donuts, buy sweet gifts, and win Glaze's heart.

  • Quest: left stick moves, right stick snap-turns. Point and pull the trigger to press buttons or grab donuts, or just touch a donut with your hand.
  • Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move, drag to look, click to interact.
  • Catch donuts at the Donut Stand (burnt ones cost 2), buy gifts in the shops, then give them to Glaze in the plaza.
